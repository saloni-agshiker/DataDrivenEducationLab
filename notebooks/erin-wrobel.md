## 02/12/2020 - Class Notes
* Data Privacy and how we can use Blockchains in education
* FERPA - passed in ‘74, protects student information
* Outreach focus groups for various specific populations
* Why does privacy matter? Because people can make a ton of money off of you
* White Paper by the Digital Credentials Consortium - creating a digital infrastructure for academic credentials
* Trust in the infrastructure/ program HAS to be there in order for it to work
* Examples: 
* Does the bartender need to know how tall you are?
* If you have a parking ticket, you can’t view your academic records
* Data silos - where you are in your learning journey determines how much/what data is collected and where it is stored, who controls it
* Who has agency/ownership over your data?
* Discussion: what can we do and how do we get people to care?
* People often don’t care until something goes wrong - you’re going to worry about the things you have the most control over and that have the most effect on your life
