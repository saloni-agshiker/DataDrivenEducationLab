  - **classified messages** (based on ML classification of messages):
      - Did not matter how many "good" comments a student posted: *"The frequencies of messages classified as 0 and 1 by ML did not significantly explain the variation in students’ final grades."*

                  - *"Moreover, on average, students scored 1.817 points higher when they posted a message endorsed by all three ML algorithms."*
  - *"Male and female students do not significantly differ on their final course grades controlling for the frequencies of unclassified or machine classified messages."*
