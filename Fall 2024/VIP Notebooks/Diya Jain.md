ML for Mass Generating Posts:
- Language Models (GPT-2, GPT-3, T5): Generate human-like text based on input prompts. Fine-tune on specific datasets to align with your domain.
- Auto-regressive Models (GPT): Predict the next word in a sequence, ideal for long-form content like blog posts or social media.
- Seq2Seq Models (T5, BART): Useful for structured content generation, such as text summarization or product descriptions.
