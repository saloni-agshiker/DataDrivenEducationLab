### ***Face to Face Meeting on Wednesday***
* To further express the purpose and significance of our work, we were presented on recent trends in learning, including:
  * how learning has become considered to be "continual, lifetime," with learning and work no longer being considered seperate
  * how we continually acquire new sources of info every day, using new technologies and tools to define our thinking
  * and how this then led to the growth in sample saizes of educational data for researchers in this field, leading to big data analytics
* We then were presented on the various data sources offered to us during this project, these being edX and canvas (potentially piazza too). This was a very important topic, as it dictates what technology we can use and defines what our goals are for this semester.
* We were also lectured on data security,as we are dealing with confidential data and private information that is protected by FERPA. As a result, to secure this data we must:
  * keep as much data on C21U servers as possible
  * ensure data is not being backed up to any third party
  * ensure data is securely encrypted
  * and use aggregations for data when possible
* Finally we were presented on some of the technology that we might be using. This included pSQL and MongoDB, which are commonly used for databases.
