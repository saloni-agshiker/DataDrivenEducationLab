Interview with James (From JITI team):

- Key points:
  - Use case of discussion forums to understand TA Contributions
  - Utility of the title is an interesting indicator
  - Volume of posts doesn't always imply better engagement or understanding
  - Prioritization would be helpful => Trends and sentiments can help here, try to observe spikes in activity on a thread
  - Cognitive presence in conjuction with topic modeling and sentiments trends can be a way to look forward
  - If a thread's blowing up, maybe alert the TAs by customising the notifications => Assigning it to a TA who's historically been more active during that time
  - Identifying duplicates
  - Most threads activity dies down after 3 days
  - Answering good questions and real concerns is rewarding, but 25-30% is a case of not Reading The Dang Instructions (RTDI), which is frustrating for the TAs
  - Identifying the most helpful students can be an interesting usecase to explore as well, and observing social networks and activity for different topics
  - Student preparedness is another thing that could be looked at using their posts on forum => Topic analysis student wise
