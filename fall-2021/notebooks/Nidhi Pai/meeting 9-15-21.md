### Process of Research

6 steps of research: identify problem, review literature, specify a purpose for research, (these are on the first presentation) collect data, analyze data, reporting and evaluating research

Types of research: exploratory, descriptive, relational, explanatory (cause/effect)

Pro tip: focus on documentation so that the work can continue easily

### UX Research Process

UX research - the process of adopting various research methods to identify the needs and interests of users with regards to a problem space/product to guide the design process

Steps: Listen to users, generate ideas for design, create a prototype, interact with users to evaluate design

Attitudinal vs. behavioral -> attitudinal is listening to what others say and behavioral is watching how they interact with the tool
