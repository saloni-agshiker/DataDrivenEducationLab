## Predicting satisfaction with MOOCs as a metric of MOOC success

- Traditionally, success is measured in terms of completion rate, but actually this is not a good metric because many learners have goals other than completion - for example only learning some specific topic
- Satisfaction is more useful from a business POV
- This study tries to predict satisfaction (based on reviews) based on learner sentiment toward the course (from review text with NLP) and course attributes
- Important learner factors:  assessment, instructor, and content
  - assessment: want clear instructions, meaningfully connected to lessons, and challenges that apply the content rather than just recall or busywork type assignments
- Important course factors: schedule - self paced or preset
