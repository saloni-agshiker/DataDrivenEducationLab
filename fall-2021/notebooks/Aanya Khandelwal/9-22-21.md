### Project Management

- Project management is key to success so there need to be clear deadlines and milestones
- Two types of management:
  - Waterfall - Single iteration and all done at once
  - Agile - Many interations, multiple feedbacks and always up-to-date
- Can use either Trello or Jira for Agile management
- Important things to consider:
  - Assigning clear roles and tasks
  - Get feedback often
  - Communicate regularly
